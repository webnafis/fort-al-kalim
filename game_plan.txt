0. THE STORY — FORT AL-KALIM (قلعة الكليم)
=========================================

  THE LEGEND OF FORT AL-KALIM

  Long ago, in an age when scholars were the mightiest warriors and
  ink was worth more than blood, there existed an ancient fortress
  unlike anything the world had ever seen.

  Fort Al-Kalim — قلعة الكليم — The Fortress of the Word.

  It was not built with stone or iron. Its walls were raised from
  thousand-year-old manuscripts, its towers carved from scrolls of
  Arabic poetry, and its gates sealed shut by the sacred weight of
  language itself. No sword could breach it. No army could conquer it.
  Only one force in the world had the power to bring those walls down —

  A word, spoken correctly.

  The fortress stood between two great kingdoms: one that spoke in
  Arabic, and one that reached across the world to understand it.
  For centuries, neither kingdom could cross to the other side.
  The walls held firm — not from strength, but from ignorance.

  The ancient Keepers of Al-Kalim declared a sacred law:

    "Weapons of steel shall not open these gates.
     Only the warrior who masters our words
     shall ever break our walls.
     For a word understood is a fortress fallen.
     A word forgotten is a wall rebuilt."

  And so the War of Words began.

  Champions entered the arena from both sides — armed not with arrows,
  but with vocabulary. Not with shields, but with memory. They hurled
  missiles of meaning at each other's forts: each correct translation
  a direct hit, each mastered word a crack in the enemy wall.

  The warrior who stops learning watches their fort crumble.
  The warrior who masters every word... watches the gates of Al-Kalim
  swing open before them.

  Generation after generation, the duel continues.
  New champions rise. New levels are unlocked. New words are learned.
  And with every word mastered, the fortress yields a little more.

  Today, you step into the arena.
  Your fort stands at 200. Your enemy is ready.
  Your words are your only weapons.

  Will you break their walls before they break yours?

  — Welcome to Fort Al-Kalim. —

=========================================
2. USER ACCOUNT & PROGRESSION SYSTEM
=========================================
  - New user creates an account (Google Play or email)
  - Every user starts at Level 1
  - Each level has 100 Arabic-English word pairs
  - FORT STRENGTH: Fixed at 200 HP for ALL levels (no HP scaling)
  - Fort visual design updates per level (aesthetic upgrade only)
  - Weapon/missile design updates per level (aesthetic upgrade only)

  LEVEL-UP RULE:
    - Player levels up when ALL words in ALL 4 sections reach 0 AP
    - UI and framing: focused on WINS (looks like a war game)
    - Behind the scenes: mastery drives progression, not win count
    - Win/loss record tracked and displayed but does NOT affect level-up

  SCORE:
    - Lifetime score = total cumulative damage dealt in all games ever
    - Every successful attack adds its damage value to lifetime score
    - Failed attacks add 0

=========================================
3. MATCHMAKING SYSTEM
=========================================

  WHAT IS "TOTAL REMAINING AP"?
    - Sum of ALL current attack power values across all 100 words,
      all 4 sections, in a player's current level
    - HIGH = hasn't learned much yet (newer/weaker)
    - LOW = close to mastering the level (experienced)
    - Used ONLY as a tiebreaker within the same level

  MATCHMAKING RULES (in order):

    RULE 1 — SAME LEVEL FIRST (highest priority):
      If 2+ players of the SAME level are in the queue:
        → Match them together, ALWAYS, no matter their AP difference
        → Among multiple same-level candidates: pick pair with SMALLEST
          total AP difference (most balanced skill within that level)
        → Further tie: pick whoever has been waiting longest

    RULE 2 — CROSS-LEVEL MATCH (only if alone at their level):
      If a player is the ONLY one at their level in the queue:
        → Compare with players from NEAREST levels (L-1 and L+1 first,
          then L-2 / L+2, etc.)
        → Among cross-level candidates: pick the pair with SMALLEST
          total AP difference (closest skill level)
        → Further tie: pick whoever has waited longest

    RULE 3 — 15-SECOND HARD CAP:
      If a player has waited 15 seconds AND queue size >= 2:
        → Immediately match with best available (smallest AP diff)
        → Level gap is ignored at this point
      If waited 15 seconds AND queue size < 2 (alone):
        → Offer AI opponent fallback

  AI OPPONENT:
    - Mimics a player with similar total remaining AP
    - AI difficulty calibrated to human's skill
    - Available instantly as fallback option at any time

=========================================
4. FAIRNESS IN CROSS-LEVEL MATCHES
=========================================
  - Fort HP fixed at 200 for all levels → no HP imbalance ever
  - Different word pools per level → NOT a fairness problem:
      * Each player's words selected from THEIR OWN level's pool
      * Word selection is independent per player
      * Both players always start with equivalent attack potential (200)
  - AP difference check applied cross-level to ensure skill balance

=========================================
5. WORD SELECTION ALGORITHM (Per Game)
=========================================
  MASTERY TRACKING:
    - Each word has 4 independent AP values (one per section)
    - High AP = not mastered yet → selected first for games
    - 0 AP = fully mastered → used as bonus words only

  WORD SELECTION (per player, per game):
    STEP 1: Per section, rank player's 100 words by current AP (high first)
    STEP 2: Select words to fill each section's damage quota
    STEP 3: AIM for equal word count across all 4 sections
    STEP 4: Each player's word list built independently (P1 ≠ P2)
    STEP 5: Same word CAN appear in multiple sections (tracked separately)

  ATTACK POWER BALANCE FORMULA:
    Fort HP = 200. Player's total attack potential must also = 200.
    Try for equal words per section. Multipliers: See=1.0, Listen=1.25,
    Write=1.5, Speak=1.75. Sum of multipliers = 5.5.

    Let N = words per section, B = base damage per word:
      B × N × 5.5 = 200 → B = 200 / (N × 5.5)

    For N=10 (default, 40 words total):
      B = 3.636
      See damage:    10 × 3.636 × 1.00 = 36.36
      Listen damage: 10 × 3.636 × 1.25 = 45.45
      Write damage:  10 × 3.636 × 1.50 = 54.55
      Speak damage:  10 × 3.636 × 1.75 = 63.64
      TOTAL = 200 ✓

    Per-hit damage breakdown (one word used once):
      Hit 1: B × M × 1.00  (full)
      Hit 2: B × M × 0.75  (75%)
      Hit 3: B × M × 0.50  (50%)
      Hit 4: B × M × 0.25  (25%)
      Word exhausted after 4 hits.

    Unequal distribution: if a section has fewer words available,
    B is auto-adjusted so total across all sections still = 200.

  OVERFLOW RULE (not enough high-AP words):
    - If player's unmastered words sum < 200, add bonus mastered words
    - Bonus words reset to full B power as level-completion reward
    - Bonus words LOCKED until all real unfinished words hit 0 AP

=========================================
6. PRE-GAME: READING PHASE
=========================================
  - Before combat: players enter optional reading phase
  - Can review all assigned words:
      English text, Arabic text, spelling, audio, speaking practice
  - Fully optional — can skip entirely
  - No time limit
  - Player who finishes first can start attacking IMMEDIATELY
  - Other player can still be reading — NOT blocked
  - No return to reading once combat phase begins

=========================================
7. THE 4 ATTACK SECTIONS (Bombs/Missiles)
=========================================
  Section       | Multiplier | Style
  --------------|------------|---------------------------
  See (Image)   | 1.0x       | Visual recognition
  Listen (Audio)| 1.25x      | Auditory recognition
  Write (Tiles) | 1.5x       | Construction / arrangement
  Speak (Voice) | 1.75x      | Active production / recall

  ATTACK DEGRADATION (all sections):
    - Successful hit: damage = current AP × M; AP decreases by B×0.25
    - Failed attempt: 0 damage; word LOCKED for 30 seconds
    - Words sorted: highest current AP first (most powerful at top)
    - No time limit per attack attempt (rush comes from the live duel)

  SECTION 1 — LISTEN:
    Shown   : Arabic audio plays + 4 English text options (1 correct)
    Action  : Tap the correct English meaning
    Success : Missile fires, damage dealt, AP degrades
    Fail    : No missile, word locked 30s

  SECTION 2 — SEE:
    Shown   : Image of the Arabic word + 4 Arabic text options (1 correct)
    Action  : Tap the correct Arabic word
    Images  : Concrete nouns → real photo/illustration
              Abstract words → symbolic icon or visual metaphor
    Success : Missile fires, damage dealt, AP degrades
    Fail    : No missile, word locked 30s

  SECTION 3 — SPEAK:
    Shown   : English word on screen + microphone activated
    Action  : Speak the Arabic translation aloud
    Validate: Arabic speech recognition checks pronunciation
    Success : Missile fires, damage dealt, AP degrades
    Fail    : No missile, word locked 30s
    Disabled: If speech recognition unavailable → visible but greyed out
              Notice: "Voice section unavailable on this device"

  SECTION 4 — WRITE:
    Shown   : English translation + Arabic word as rearrangeable tiles
    Tiles   : Short words → individual Arabic letters
              Longer words → small chunk tiles (syllable groups)
              Rule: tiles concatenated in correct order = exact Arabic word
                    (including harakat, diacritics, all symbols)
              No extra/distractor tiles — only the correct tiles shown
    Action  : Drag/tap tiles into correct order
    Success : Missile fires, damage dealt, AP degrades
    Fail    : No missile, word locked 30s

8. WORD DATABASE DESIGN
=========================================
  SOURCE:
    - User provides JSON files (100 words per level)
    - Imported into Firebase Firestore + cached locally

  WORD RECORD:
    * arabic_text     : string (full Arabic with harakat)
    * english_text    : string
    * audio_url       : Firebase Storage ref (Arabic pronunciation)
    * image_url       : Firebase Storage ref (for SEE section)
    * write_tiles     : string[] (chunks, concat = exact arabic_text)

  PER-USER PER-WORD-PER-SECTION (Firestore + local SQLite):
    * current_ap_see / listen / write / speak : float
    * lock_until_see / listen / write / speak  : timestamp | null
    * is_bonus_word                             : bool

  STORAGE:
    - Word content → Firebase Storage + local cache
    - User progress → Firestore + local SQLite (offline sync)
    - Live game state → Firebase Realtime Database
    - Leaderboard → Firestore

12. DIRECT FRIEND MATCH (Play With Friends)
=========================================
  PURPOSE:
    Two specific players want to duel each other directly,
    bypassing the algorithmic matchmaking queue entirely.

  TWO WAYS TO START A FRIEND MATCH:

  WAY 1 — ROOM CODE (Quick Invite):
    HOW IT WORKS:
      1. Player A taps "Play with Friend" → "Create Room"
      2. App generates a unique 6-character ROOM CODE (e.g. "KL-7842")
         and displays it on screen
      3. Player A shares the code with friend via any messaging app
         (WhatsApp, SMS, copy-paste — whatever they prefer)
      4. Player B opens Fort Al-Kalim → "Play with Friend" → "Enter Code"
      5. Player B types the code → instantly connected to Player A's room
      6. Both see each other's name and level → tap "Ready" → game starts

    TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION:
      - Room created as a node in Firebase Realtime Database:
          rooms / {code} / { hostId, guestId, status, level, timestamp }
      - Code expires after 5 minutes if no one joins
      - Once both players ready → word selection runs → reading phase starts
      - Works cross-level (two friends at different levels can play)

  WAY 2 — FRIEND INVITE (From Social Graph):
    HOW IT WORKS:
      1. Player A opens "Friends" tab → sees their GPGS friends list
      2. Taps on a friend → taps "Challenge to Duel"
      3. Friend receives a PUSH NOTIFICATION:
         "[PlayerA] has challenged you to a duel in Fort Al-Kalim! Accept?"
      4. Friend taps "Accept" in notification → opens game → drops into
         a private room with Player A
      5. Both see each other → tap "Ready" → game starts

    TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION:
      - Invite notification sent via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — FREE
      - Invite stored in Firestore: invites/{recipientId}/{inviteId}
      - Recipient's app listens for new invites in real time
      - Invite expires after 2 minutes if not accepted
      - Accepted invite creates the same Room structure as Way 1

  FRIEND MATCH RULES:
    - Word selection and AP balance formula: same as algorithmic match
    - No 15-second timeout — friends wait for each other willingly
    - Cross-level play allowed (friends at different levels can duel)
    - Fort HP = 200 for both players as always
    - Room Code mode: host can also cancel the room at any time
